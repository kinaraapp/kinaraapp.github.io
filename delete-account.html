Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. Here's everything you need to know before proceeding.

This Action Is Permanent

Deleting your account is irreversible. All your data, progress, and achievements will be permanently erased and cannot be recovered. Please consider carefully before proceeding.

1

Open the Bingwa App

Launch the Bingwa app on your device and log in to the account you wish to delete.

2

Navigate to Settings

Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner of the home screen to open Settings.

3

Select "Account"

Scroll down and tap on "Account" to view your account management options.

4

Tap "Delete Account"

At the bottom of the Account page, tap "Delete Account". You will be asked to confirm your identity by entering your password.

5

Confirm Deletion

Read the final warning and tap "Permanently Delete". Your account will be deactivated immediately and fully deleted within 90 days.

What You Will Lose

All XP & Levels
Your accumulated progress is erased
Daily Streaks
Your hard-earned streaks reset to zero
Leaderboard Rankings
You will be removed from all leaderboards
Badges & Achievements
All earned badges are permanently removed
Study Notes
All personal notes and highlights deleted
Subscription Access
Premium features end; no refund issued

Deletion Timeline

0

Immediate — Account Deactivated

Your account is immediately deactivated. You cannot log in, and your profile is hidden from leaderboards and battles.

30

Day 30 — Grace Period

You can still contact support to restore your account if you change your mind within the first 30 days.

90

Day 90 — Permanent Deletion

All personal data, progress, and content are permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers.
